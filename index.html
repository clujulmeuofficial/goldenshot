Privacy Policy

Your photography. Your privacy.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GoldenShot ("the App"), developed by Vereș Gabriel, may process information when you use its photography features. GoldenShot helps users discover golden hour and blue hour times, save and search locations, view weather forecasts, and receive AI-assisted photography advice.

Please read this policy together with the permissions and disclosures presented by the App.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the features you use and the App's implementation, the following information may be processed:

  • Location information: your device location, if location permission is requested and granted, or a location that you enter manually.
  • Searched and saved locations: place names, coordinates, and favorite locations that you search for or save.
  • AI requests: questions, photography scenarios, and other text you submit to receive suggested camera settings.
  • Weather requests: location information needed to retrieve a forecast.
  • Technical information: information that may be processed by the operating system or third-party services, such as diagnostic or service logs, depending on the SDKs used.

The exact information processed depends on the permissions, services, and software components included in the published version.

3. Location Information

Location information may be used to calculate golden hour and blue hour times and to provide location-specific weather information. You may also be able to enter or search for a location manually.

Important: whether precise device coordinates are collected, transmitted to a provider, or processed only on your device depends on the App's actual implementation. Verify these details before publishing this policy.

You can manage location permissions through your Android device settings. Disabling location access may limit some features.

4. AI Photography Assistance

GoldenShot may use Google Gemini to generate photography advice and suggested camera settings. When you submit a request, the text and any accompanying information required to answer it may be sent to the AI service.

Avoid including passwords, financial details, sensitive personal information, or other information that is not needed for your photography question.

The processing, retention, and use of submitted information by the provider are subject to the provider's applicable terms and privacy documentation. Review the actual integration and current provider terms before release.

Google privacy information: Google Privacy Policy .

5. Weather and Location Services

The App may contact third-party weather or place-search providers to retrieve forecasts or location results. These requests may include a place name or geographic coordinates.

The exact providers and the information shared with each provider must be confirmed from the App's code and configuration. Their respective privacy policies may also apply.

6. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Calculate golden hour and blue hour times.
  • Find, display, and remember locations.
  • Retrieve location-specific weather forecasts.
  • Generate AI-assisted photography suggestions.
  • Operate, maintain, and protect the App and its services.

7. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Saved locations and preferences may be stored locally on your device or by a storage service, depending on the implementation. Information sent to third-party providers may be processed or retained according to their applicable policies and settings.

To remove saved locations stored locally, use any available controls in the App or clear the App's data through Android settings. Clearing App data may remove locally stored preferences.

If the App uses cloud storage or third-party services, additional deletion steps may be necessary. Confirm the actual storage architecture and deletion behavior before publishing.

8. Sharing with Third Parties

GoldenShot does not sell your personal information as a business practice. However, information may be transmitted to service providers when required for features you use, such as AI responses, weather forecasts, or place searches.

Which providers receive information depends on the services and SDKs actually integrated into the App. Do not publish this statement until it has been checked against the implementation.

9. Advertising and Accounts

GoldenShot is currently intended to operate without advertisements and without requiring users to create an account or sign in.

This description must be updated if advertising, authentication, analytics, or other data-processing features are added.

10. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information handled by the App and its service providers. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The security measures that apply depend on the App's actual architecture and the providers it uses.

11. Children's Privacy

GoldenShot is intended for photographers and photography enthusiasts. This policy does not, by itself, establish whether the App is designed for children or whether special child-directed privacy requirements apply.

If the App is made available to children or is included in a child-directed audience, its data practices, store listing, permissions, and third-party SDKs must be reviewed for the applicable requirements before release.

12. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can manage Android permissions, including location access, through your device settings. You can also choose what information you include in AI requests.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Contact the developer to make a privacy-related request.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at the URL where this policy is hosted. Please review it periodically.
