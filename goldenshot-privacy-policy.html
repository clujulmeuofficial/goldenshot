Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GoldenShot (“the app”) may handle information when you use its photography-planning features, including golden hour and blue hour times, saved and searched locations, AI-assisted camera recommendations, and a three-day weather forecast.

1. Information and permissions

Location: With your permission, GoldenShot may use your device’s current location to provide location-specific times and forecasts. You can manually refresh the location. You can manage or revoke location permission in your device settings. The precise handling of location data depends on the app’s implementation and the services it contacts.

Saved and searched locations: You can search for places and save locations for later use. Confirm whether saved locations remain only on your device or are sent to or stored by an external service before publishing this policy.

AI camera assistant: When you submit a question to the AI assistant, the text and any other information included in that request may be sent to Google Gemini or the configured AI provider to generate a response. Avoid including personal or sensitive information in prompts.

Weather and place information: The app may contact third-party services to retrieve forecasts, place-search results, or related information. The providers and information sent to them should be identified accurately in the final policy.

2. Accounts and advertising

GoldenShot does not require users to create an account and, according to the current app plan, does not display advertisements.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to calculate golden hour and blue hour times, show location-specific weather information, search for places, remember saved locations, and answer requests made to the AI camera assistant. The app should collect or transmit only the information necessary for these functions.

4. Third-party services

GoldenShot may use Google services, including Google Gemini, and other providers for features such as place search and weather forecasts. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. The final version of this policy should name the actual providers and link to their current privacy information once the project has been checked.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

Information may be processed on your device or by external services, depending on how the app is implemented. Before publishing, confirm where saved locations and AI requests are stored, whether any server logs are retained, and how users can delete locally saved information or request deletion of information held by the developer.

6. Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

GoldenShot is intended for a general photography audience and is not specifically designed for children. If children are included in the app’s target audience, the app and all integrated services must comply with applicable child-privacy requirements and the relevant Google Play Families policies. The app’s actual target audience and data practices must be confirmed before publication.

8. Your choices

You can manage location permission through your Android device settings. You can choose whether to use the AI assistant. You should also be able to remove saved locations using the app’s available controls, if supported by the final implementation.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features, providers, or data practices change. The current version will be published at this page, with its latest update date.
